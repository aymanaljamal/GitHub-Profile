// 01 — about

Building systems that hold up under real use.

I'm a Computer Science student at Birzeit University (2022 – 2026), focused on software engineering, backend development, databases, mobile applications and the fundamentals of AI/ML. My work sits at the intersection of backend engineering and mobile development — designing the API and data layer a product runs on, then building the Flutter or Android client that people actually use.

During my internship at LogesTechs, I worked hands-on with Spring Boot, SQL-driven systems, Linux environments and AWS deployment fundamentals, and got early exposure to prompt engineering and AI-assisted features. Outside of internships, I design and ship complete systems on my own — from Student Bag, a university companion app, to ERP backends for warehouse and logistics management.

I care about clean architecture, dependable APIs and interfaces that don't get in the way — and I enjoy the problem-solving side of engineering as much as the building itself: tracing a bug to its root cause, tightening a slow query, or reworking a data model so the next feature is easy instead of hard.
